The speech balloon over the counter — a regular calling their order in counter slang, or the relay boy shouting it down to the kitchen.

```jsx
<Shout open={!!line}>واحد كبير بورد زيادة</Shout>
```

A proper comic balloon: paper fill, ink outline, and a two-layer tail so the outline reads all the way round. This is where the slang lives — "one large with extra ward" is how a regular actually orders, and the balloon is the only place the player hears it before the slip translates it.
