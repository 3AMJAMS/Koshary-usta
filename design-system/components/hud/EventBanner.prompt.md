The morning newspaper beat — one headline a day, centred over the stage before the shop opens, then dismissed.

```jsx
<EventBanner
  kicker={t('news')}
  title="Onions have jumped"
  detail="The price of onions doubled overnight. Frying costs double today." />
```

Paper card, not a dark panel — the day's news is a physical thing on the counter. There are ten events in the source (calm, onion, payday, bus, gas, ramadan, school, critic, heat, feast) and each one names a real pressure an Egyptian koshary shop feels. Write `detail` as consequence in plain words, never as a stat line: "The flame is weak today. Every cooking window is tighter." — not "cooking band ×0.72".
