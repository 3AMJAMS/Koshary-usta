The day's rating on the end-of-shift card — three stars, earned against the chapter's daily money goal.

```jsx
<StarRating value={2} />
```

Typographic ★, not drawn art: lit stars get a 2.5px ink stroke plus a warm glow, unlit ones sit dimmed at 78% scale so the player sees the gap. They pop 160ms apart, which is the game's one moment of ceremony — don't shorten it.
